#SIS vs. LMS: What's the Difference?#
While often integrated, an SIS and a Learning Management System (LMS) serve distinct functions:

SIS (Student Information System): Focuses on administrative data—enrollment, tuition, attendance, transcripts, and official grades.

LMS (Learning Management System): Focuses on day-to-day instruction—delivering course material, collecting assignments, running quizzes, and facilitating classroom discussions (e.g., Canvas, Google Classroom, Moodle).
